📄 Privacy & Terms

Ori is a small, independent project — not a company product, built and run by one person for a small group of people. This isn't a law-firm-written policy, just a plain explanation of what actually happens.

What's stored. Your messages, chat titles, and (if you're signed in) your name/email from Google are stored in our database to make chat history, memory, and the app itself work. Guest sessions are tied to a temporary anonymous ID instead of an account.

Who else sees your messages. To generate replies, your messages are sent to third-party AI providers — Google (Gemini) and Groq. Depending on which free-tier plan is active, these providers may process or, in some cases, use free-tier data to improve their own models — this is standard for free-tier AI APIs generally, not something unique to Ori. Please don't share anything here you wouldn't want a third party to possibly see — passwords, financial details, medical records, or anything similarly sensitive.

Uploaded files. Images, videos, or files you attach are sent to whichever AI provider is handling that message (always Gemini for files, since Groq's model is text-only) so it can understand them. The raw file isn't permanently stored in our database — only the text of the conversation is kept.

What Ori is not. Ori isn't a medical, legal, or financial professional, and its advice shouldn't replace one. Please use your own judgment, especially for anything serious.

Your control. You can export any chat as a text file, delete individual chats, or delete your entire account (which removes your messages, memory, and usage data) at any time from Settings.

Who can use this. Ori is meant for a small, known group of people, not the general public. Don't use it for anything illegal, harmful, or abusive — accounts can be removed for misuse.

No ads, no selling data. Ori doesn't run ads and doesn't sell your data to anyone.

Changes. Since this is a small, evolving project, this page may change as features change. Nothing here is a permanent guarantee — if something about how your data is handled matters a lot to you, ask directly rather than assuming.
